# Driver-Pass
Projects and assignments from my SNHU classes

//Briefly summarize the DriverPass project. Who was the client? What type of system did they want you to design?

This project was about designing a system for DriverPass, a driving school. The goal was to create something where students can book lessons, take practice tests, and track their progress, while staff can manage everything on their side.

//What did you do particularly well?

I think I did a solid job taking what the client wanted and turning it into clear diagrams and a system that actually makes sense.

//If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?

If I went back, I’d probably clean up my UML diagrams a bit more and add a little more detail so they’re easier to read.

//How did you interpret the user’s needs and implement them into your system design? Why is it so important to consider the user’s needs when designing?

I focused on making things simple for users, like easy booking and tracking progress. That matters because if it’s confusing, people just won’t use it.

//How do you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a system?

I usually break everything down into smaller pieces first, then map it out with diagrams before building anything. It helps keep things organized and easier to understand.
